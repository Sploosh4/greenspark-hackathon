# Namash statistical outlook

Season Ahead can display a separately validated frozen export from Namash’s Ridge + LightGBM model alongside the available ECMWF source. ECMWF remains selected on first load. Choose Forecast source, then the statistical issue and map measure. Source/issue changes clear the applied warm-month filter; review the six chosen months and apply again. Editable threshold and count remain planning settings, not validated hazard thresholds.

The new authenticated read-only route /api/q3/statistical-outlook uses contract season-ahead-statistical-outlook-v1. It checks bounded bytes against the published SHA-256, then validates coordinates, issue/valid months, array shapes, units, probabilities, intervals, metadata and rounded anomaly consistency. It never executes a pipeline or loads a serialised model. Invalid replacement retains the process’s last validated export; cold failure is unavailable. The four-source ECMWF/business snapshot contract and updater are unchanged.

The first day used internally as a display month anchor is never presented as a verified release date.

Monthly mean T2M is °C. The anomaly and chance-warmer measures use NASA POWER/MERRA-2’s matching 1991–2020 calendar-month mean. Keep this separate from ECMWF’s 1993–2016 model hindcast and SILO’s historical 1991–2020 midpoint normal. Export rounding is 0.1°C for temperature, normal and interval; 0.01 for anomaly and probability. Warm-month counts use these exported anomaly values, including equality, without inventing unrounded ML values.

Probabilities and the published 80% model range are experimental. The range is a predicted mean ±1.2816 times the per-lead hindcast error spread. It is neither daily extremes nor a verified confidence claim. Final isotonic calibration was fitted to 2011–2025 hindcast outcomes; the supplied raw-probability reliability table is not independent held-out validation of that calibration. Published cell skill is uncorrected ensemble MSE skill against climatology for 2006–2025, not current-issue confidence. No scientific superiority, blend, heatwave probability, account demand prediction or individual risk score is claimed.
