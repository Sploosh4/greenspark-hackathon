# Season Ahead planning

Season Ahead combines a WA climate map, named planning areas, monthly temperature context, historical layers and dated synthetic account/financial context. Business uploads stage two files and require review plus explicit Replace. Financial intake separately stages eight matched files and requires another deliberate Replace. Invalid or stale proposals retain last-good state.

Accepted source `04e13ebca90c239295a59ca2ef938776e1df063cf2f08f58374c9605fb297684` includes the reviewed Perth shortcut and reusable `q3-synthetic-financial-v1` validation. Valid fresh packages no longer need a per-seed registry edit. All four legacy exact-package pins and rejection of invalid/undeclared input remain. The server checks source/cohort/geography bindings, measured sizes/rows, joins, lifecycle histories, instalment totals and cleared-grant/payment reconciliation; rehashing invalid content does not grant admission. Fresh v3 envelopes and legacy v2 context remain distinct.

The declaration, source hashes and seed are uploader-provided lineage, not authenticated generator origin. Complete internally consistent synthetic tables do not establish real-world completeness, complete daily history or arbitrary financial-system compatibility. The [contract](SYNTHETIC-FINANCIAL-PACKAGE-CONTRACT-v1.json) and [technical intake notes](FINANCIAL-INTAKE.md) define accepted schemas and resource bounds.

The current native data remains 50,000 retained / 46,500 active accounts across 385 areas at 30 June 2025. Fresh 1k and 50k cohorts at 30 September 2026 passed isolated actual-handler and browser uploads; the selected fresh 50k has 11 populated areas and is not the live 385-area snapshot. Source promotion does not activate a new dataset. Forecast month does not change dated account activity, and sparse usage is not a complete daily history or accuracy fixture.

The map shows six individual ECMWF51 monthly means for October 2026–March 2027 at native 1° cells. Warm-month filtering selects explicit months; it does not add three-/six-month aggregate forecast products or daily heatwave probabilities. External SILO history comprises 24 actual months, 12 matching-month five-year averages and 12 observational normals, separately labelled from ECMWF air-temperature/hindcast anomalies. Financial/income coverage remains source-limited. Perth selects its named area rather than a different locality sharing the coarse cell.
