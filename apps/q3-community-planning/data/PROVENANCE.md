# Season Ahead input provenance

These are the unchanged normalised inputs approved in the local Q3 preview on 28 October 2026. `inputs/manifest.json` binds their exact bytes. The backend accepts this reviewed v1 pack and validates it before activation. No raw customer records are served; business data contains historical synthetic aggregates only.

- **Monthly means:** Copernicus C3S seasonal-monthly-single-levels, ECMWF system 51, monthly 2 m air temperature ensemble mean, issue 1 October 2026, valid October 2026–March 2027. Absolute K converted to °C during the verified decode. Raw GRIB SHA-256 `490c6f4118e41008eed3528804265f376a72d2a0355b91d324a19fabce2a33b9`. [Dataset](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/seasonal-monthly-single-levels).
- **Anomalies:** C3S seasonal-postprocessed-single-levels, same model, issue, months and 1° grid. All 2,484 values were independently decoded and checked. K differences equal Celsius differences; no 273.15 offset was subtracted. Raw hash `5ddc6a8adf1feaca185983895e8160b9b8e989a74fc47145ea257aee4a84d9f5`. Reference: matching 1993–2016 model hindcast climate, documented and not independently recomputed. [Dataset](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/seasonal-postprocessed-single-levels), [method](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/CKB/pages/340775655/C3S+seasonal+forecast+product+descriptions).
- **History:** Queensland Government SILO, underlying Bureau of Meteorology observations. Twelve actual interpolated locality sample points; calendar-month mean daily highs and lows over 1991–2020. Original raw-file hashes are retained per point. These values are not a statewide historical layer or the forecast anomaly baseline. [SILO](https://www.longpaddock.qld.gov.au/silo/).
- **Accounts:** GreenSpark Q1 Demo CANDIDATES Historical 2023–2025, supplied 28 October 2026. Active at the latest supplied actual usage snapshot, 30 June 2025: 393 accounts, 142 prepaid and 251 billed. Of 448 retained rows, 55 closed accounts are excluded and zero have unknown status. Account open/close dates were corroborated with actual snapshot usage. These are historical synthetic accounts, not current 2026 or forecast-month activity. Source customer SHA-256 `023b83dbb62aa9853120f71735512c5a64810bb6ff31007a9c63811804211011`; usage/context hashes remain in the JSON.
- **Income:** Existing verified 2021 ABS Census POA context, exact year/geography/code joins. Fitzroy POA6765 is $1,226 median weekly household income. This describes an area, not an account. [ABS source](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/POA6765).

MapLibre GL JS and its licence are bundled unchanged from the reviewed demo. MapTiler Streets v4 resources remain proxied at runtime; MapTiler/OpenStreetMap credits and logo stay visible. Real provider water polygons visually mask the native source grid at coastlines. No finer forecast precision, interpolation, fictitious service polygon or customer point is introduced.

No CDS token is present in these files or requested by this application. The source retrieval/GRIB decoder remains a separate preparation step. C3S data credits and source links remain in the interface and these notes; the source pack records CC BY 4.0 and the additional licence for non-European contributions. No new licence acceptance occurred in this integration task.

## Namash statistical source

The separately pinned export is described in [NAMASH-OUTLOOK.md](../NAMASH-OUTLOOK.md). Original source/export repository commit `e8143867c295cac0071f6e6f90ee5e0295960a95` and export SHA256 `909904199eafcefcfaaa8844ae8bf7a4b53ca55326df3a2973290f294e7dc247` are retained with the MIT notice under `statistical/`. Acknowledge Namash / GreenSpark, NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project (MERRA-2), and NOAA CPC Niño 3.4/AAO indices. The original provenance also lists a Natural Earth outline; that outline is not imported or used here. The app keeps its existing ABS WA boundary and SILO credits. Upstream provenance is a historical source record, not a promise that every upstream repository file is bundled.

The statistical product uses NASA POWER/MERRA-2 1991–2020 monthly climatology. Do not relabel it as the ECMWF hindcast baseline, SILO daily extremes or validated heatwave probability. No new upstream retrieval, training or licence acceptance occurs in this local source update.
