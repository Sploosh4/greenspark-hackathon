Schema, dates and retained information

The source columns remain unchanged. This workflow uses customer ID, postcode/locality, prepayment flag, opening/closure dates, and dated daily usage evidence. It validates daily usage numbers and supply status. Other customer attributes are not used or persisted. Approximate locality points come from the pinned GeoNames/SILO registry, with genuine ABS WA boundary checks; they are not customer coordinates or retailer coverage.

A recorded closure on the snapshot date counts as closed. A known later opening is retained separately as “not open at snapshot”. A disconnected supply row does not by itself close an account. Blank opening dates or absent snapshot-day usage are unknown.

A stage expires after 30 minutes and is discarded on server restart. Replacing requires the active generation to match the one reviewed. Re-stage if another replacement or source refresh has changed it. Raw files, individual customer IDs and clinical/support fields are never written by this uploader.
