Monthly mean forecast / 2 m air temperature · ECMWF system 51.

Monthly ensemble mean, not a daily peak or heatwave probability. A 1° cell is spatial resolution, not ±1°C error.

Native 1° cells use unchanged source values. The existing ABS ASGS 2021 WA display boundary masks coverage outside WA; underlying forecast source coverage includes ocean. Its 0.002° display simplification retains every source island and ring. External basemap roads and town text supplement the local view when available. Business counts use the dated snapshot shown above and stay unchanged when browsing forecast months.

Warm-month filter: C3S monthly ensemble-mean anomalies use the matching ECMWF system 51 model hindcast mean for 1993–2016, matched by start and lead time. This baseline is documented, not independently recomputed. The editable starting values are +0.5°C in 3 of all 6 months; Apply is required. Counts use unrounded source anomalies at or above the chosen Δ°C; every selected month must be present. These are monthly anomaly counts, not daily heatwaves or probabilities. SILO history remains separate. The statewide historical layer uses native 0.05° interpolated observations and nearest-cell rendering, clipped to the genuine ABS WA boundary. Coverage is October 2021–September 2026; the last 24 months and five-year matching-month averages are available. Its temperature midpoint is mean((daily Tmax + daily Tmin)/2), not ECMWF monthly mean air temperature. Five-year ranges describe five monthly means, not daily extremes. The source contains 2,623 cell-days with Tmin above Tmax; these are flagged and retained without correction. Eighteen coastal/island source cells remain missing. The optional 1991–2020 matching monthly normal is derived from SILO, with equal weight for 30 yearly complete monthly means. It retains 585 inversion cell-days; the separately labelled valid-pair sensitivity excludes suspect pairs and differs by at most 0.0092°C. This does not recalibrate forecast probabilities or replace the ECMWF 1993–2016 hindcast reference. No numeric BoM climatology is served.
